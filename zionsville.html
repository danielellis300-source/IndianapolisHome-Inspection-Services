Home Inspections in Zionsville, Indiana

Expert inspections for Boone County's most charming and prestigious community

Zionsville is unlike almost any other Indianapolis suburb — its charming brick-paved Main Street, tree-lined village neighborhoods, and upscale estate homes give it a character that's impossible to replicate. From craftsman bungalows and early 20th-century homes in the Village of Zionsville to sprawling newer estates in Eagle Township and the Countryside area, the housing stock here is diverse, beautiful, and requires an inspector who understands both historic construction and modern build quality.

Historic Zionsville homes in particular demand experienced eyes — aging knob-and-tube wiring, cast-iron plumbing, older sewer laterals, and foundation issues common in early Indiana construction are all things our inspectors know to look for. For newer luxury builds on larger Boone County lots, we also conduct thorough phase inspections and new construction walkthroughs. Whatever you're buying in Zionsville, you deserve a report you can act on — and that's exactly what we deliver.
