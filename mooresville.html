Home Inspections in Mooresville, Indiana

Morgan County's growing county seat with rural character and suburban appeal

Mooresville is the seat of Morgan County, situated just southwest of Indianapolis along the Indiana 67 corridor. The town has grown steadily as Indianapolis-area buyers seek more affordable housing with larger lots and a small-town atmosphere while maintaining reasonable commutes to the city. The Mooresville downtown square anchors the historic core, while residential development fans out along Indiana 67, State Road 144, and into the surrounding countryside with a mix of acreage properties and platted subdivisions.

Housing in Mooresville spans from historic 19th-century farmhouses and early 20th-century in-town homes to 1970s–1990s suburban builds and new construction. Rural and semi-rural properties often rely on private well water and septic systems that require specialized inspection beyond the standard home inspection scope.
